The AT Command response starts with +QLTS and  lands in the rx_callback,  setup to catch all modem responses. 

It triggers a call to `ec200_parse_net_time`, where we first convert the hh:mm:ss time into Seconds of Day, and then assign the respective members of dev->net_time their appropriate values. The dev->net time struct is passed to the SetTime function of the PAL to set the reference for out RTC.  ^34Wg1Zuk

This is a simple call to the function when we are logging the elec params timestamp on the system.  ^00523fRP
