We, are RhyGen Tech - a startup that operates in the deep-tech space. Started by 3 grads from IIT-B, we are now a team of 13 incredible people, building a company that aims to optimize the consumption of energy. The first prototype that we have worked upon, is a series hybrid power-train for light commercial vehicles. The core idea is centered around operating engines at their most efficient point at all times, leading to reduced operational losses of energy and enhancing their fuel consumption. 

In population dense areas like cities, the operation of diesel engines in quite stop-start in nature, with repeated breaking and acceleration, leading to an extremely inefficient operation of the engine over the complete drive cycle. By introducing a new configuration of the vehicle power-train, we have successfully managed to hybridize the operation of a Tata ACE. By intelligently switching the power source from engine to battery at points of inefficient operation, we manage to successfully boost the efficiency of our prototype from 15-17% to 27-30%. 

The best part? The technology that we are developing in not constrained to simply Automobiles. it can fit into any and every use-case where there is an engine that sits at the heart of a system. Think Diesel Gen-sets, Ships, Defense tech - we are working on solutions that can help optimize it all.
