Does anyone else like to just sit and read code, and try to piece together a mental tapestry of what is presented in front of you? IDK why but in the age of generated code, where patterns are plenty and volume of software produced in unparalleled, a particularly useful skill to have does indeed seem like the ability to craft mental models of what is going on in a module or a a driver that you see. 

I like to do that at work these days, where I get something to work, understand it on a high level to debug when things go wrong, and then sneak some time off as I can, to simply read and understand it all - and document it in markdown notes as much as I can. The plan is always to have an inherent and intuitive understanding of the code that's committed by me, and the effort is always to get as close to it as possible.
