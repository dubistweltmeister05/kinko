I turned 23 a week ago, and I don't think that I have ever felt a more subtle but impact-full change in my perception of life and people around me ever before. 

I feel a burning desire to be a quarterback instead of a running back these days. To be more like Manning and Brady instead of Jim brown or Walter Peyton. I have always been the rusher, you see. Give me the pill and I'll scorch the earth as I take it with me to the first down (so to speak). And for some damn reason, I now have a sudden urge to slow down and process things more. To pause and dwell with the process rather than to rush and get to the next milestone. I want to ask questions before I shoot instead of the other way around. 

I want to do more instead of just wish that I did more. That's been an issue with me I suppose, that I say a LOT and do a fraction. When it comes to work, people, or my interests, I feel that this has been a problem of mine for a long while now. That HAS to change at this point. The axiom of "Under-promise and over-deliver" becomes truer as I move through life and get older. 

I also have been defiling my mental sanctuary by not giving up any room for thought and boredom. Off-late, I have been filling it up with static and noise - be it YouTube videos or music. I keep telling myself that it'll help me focus, but to the contrary - it is the sole reason I have not been able to. That shows up at work and when I try to learn something too you see. Hell, I was playing The Jackie by Bas and J. Cole on my bluetooth headset as I started writing, but had to pause it to sit with my thoughts. 

Why so? Because I am afraid of what goes on in my grey matter. I am afraid of my thoughts, and the confrontation that sitting with it shall bring about. I am afraid that I'll find out the glaring flaws of mine that might be evident to the rest around me, and I am afraid of what it'll do to me and my confidence. Stupid thing to be afraid of really, and the realization of what needs to be done to get better hits a man like Vontaze Burfict hit Antonio Brown (one of the most grotesque hits in the NFL history). 

This is the start of me being a middle child when it comes to age after all, Neiter am I a "Young Adult" anymore, nor am I an "Uncle". I am somewhere in between. At an age where I HAVE to financially plan for not only my future, but also for my parent's retirement. As someone who was told at the age of 12 by my dear father that "I am the retirement plan" - the moment I have been dreading since back then is now here. In all of it's glory, and the weight that it brings with it. I have to be "responsible" now, and not just for myself, but for my old folk too. Investments, career moves, income, taxes, returns - time to worry about it all is here for me. And there's nothing to fall back on. Either I fly, or I fall. 

So what do I do about it all? I need to stop. I need to think. I need to read the field, analyze the oncoming challenges that are being thrown my way, and I need to process it all. I should be dropping back in the pocket, look for my wide receivers and my tied ends, analyze the incoming pass and edge rush that life throws at me, and I gotta choose. I gotta choose on should I thrown the ball to my wide-outs, or should I tuck the ball and run for the first down. And I need to keep doing this over and over, till I get the damn touchdown. I also need to realize that life isn't always at 4th down for me. I am allowed to have an in-completion, I am allowed to see a pass or two being dropped, hell I might survive even after throwing a pick-6. But what matters, is that I live to fight another down. 

That's what turning 23 is to me. Confronting all that I have been bottling up, all that I have been ignoring, all that I needed to get done but haven't come around doing yet. Time to live by the words of Tobey Maguire's Spiderman - "Just trying to do better".
