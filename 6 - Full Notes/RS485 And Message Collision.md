RS-485 (also known as TIA/EIA-485) is a standard interface for physical serial communication. Typical serial communication standards include RS-232 and RS-485, but RS-485 uses differential signaling and supports multi-point networks, unlike RS-232.


Since RS485 is nothing but a physical medium, it does not inherently solve message collisions. That responsibility lies with the protocol that is being implemented on top - MODBUS RTU being the most utilized one.
