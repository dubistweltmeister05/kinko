[[Twitter Posting]]

Look man, twitter is fucking amazing. It's fun as hell, and gets me a good laugh while I stay updated with what goes on in the wold. It helps me know things that happen all around the globe, regardless of the "Industry" it is in or the "Domain" that it concerns itself with. 

I have met people on this app that I never could have had access to, I have spoken to people who are so high up in life itself that it feels like a fever dream - remember the Deepinder Goyal interaction I had? Yeah. I found community on this app, who is real and calls out bullshit faster than anyone else, but who also stands by genuineness and real efforts like nobody else does. Football twitter is the funniest, NFL twitter is the most brutal, NBA Twitter is class when it comes to memes, electronics twitter is literally insane, tech twitter is ,well, tech twitter, and I love every single last bit of it all. 

People have reached out to me with their problems, trusting me to help them find a solution, and I really feel like there's no greater honor that a man can have. To be deemed trustworthy enough for my fellow brethren to look up to me and find inspiration from what I have done so far, it is a matter of privilege for me, you see. 

So when I started getting paid from this app - it was the best thing in the world. I was getting paid to have fun, and it was good money too you know. I had a month where I cleared 80K INR from twitter. That was 30k more than what my monthly pay was at that time. I was meme-ing, baiting, trolling, sharing what I learnt through engineering, and all that you have seen on my account. But deep down, it was getting a hold of me. I started to subconsciously craft my posts for more likes, reposts, shrares and comments. I would give my readers nuggets that they could comment about, or quote my post with. 

Is there something wrong with that? Not at all. That's just what optimizing for numbers looks like. But with me, it started getting addicting. See once you essentially get paid to have fun, you want to have as much "fun" as you can in a day. And it becomes a problem when that gets in the way of your day-job. 

I was, and to some extent, still am addicted to this app. And it very much is because of the fact that I am getting paid from it. On an average, I used to have about 200USD a month from twitter, and that would be more than enough to cover rent and a few more fun expenses that I would have. 

Now - why am I writing all that? Since the revenue sharing program ended, I have had my application for the new original content rewards program being stuck at "Submitted" for a week or so. I did a lot of self-evaluation in that time (the first day -I was checking my notifs and mail for an acceptance about thrice every hour, lol). It was clear to me that my account had strayed far, far away from what I wanted it to be. It was more optimized towards engagement and numbers, and in doing so, I was baiting a lot, meme-ing a lot, trolling a lot, calling out things a lot, and what not. 

The purpose of this account was for me to talk. About electronics, sport, my own wins and losses, experiences, and all that I live or see through. Somewhere in the chase for revenue, I really feel that I had lost the spirit that I started this account with. In the week that I have had, I gave up on the fact that I would ever be paid for my content again. 

So, I asked myself - "What would I post, if I never got paid for it again?"

The answer was clear. Long-ass posts, that go into detail. About things that people say that they "aren't that deep" - no as a matter of fact, they are indeed that deep. 

I want to talk about my love for electronics, fascination for hardware, passion for music, enthusiasm for life, craving of hip-hop, wants for some good football, the business that goes behind the NFL and NBA, the technicalities of table tennis and  cricket (hate the current state of it, but god I love the sport), about love and hate, about envy and jealousy, about the darkness that roots itself within me and the light I use to fight with it, and about so, so much more. 

And when I woke up today to the mail saying I am in, and my account is re-monetized - I choose to not optimize for engagement or numbers anymore. I want to optimize for community and quality of what I write. I want to get better at so much - software engineering, love, writing, sport, relationships, and the list goes on and on and on. 

Yes, I write too long, and yes, there will be that one MF with "I ain't reading allthat" meme in the comments - but I'll take it all. I want to keep writing such, and if you are still reading this, I believe you are someone who loves to read. Do follow if you haven't already, as I have a bunch of these long-ass posts that are in the drafts, which shall be dropped regularly. 

And as Kendrick Lamar said - "My rights, my wrongs, I write till I am right with God".
